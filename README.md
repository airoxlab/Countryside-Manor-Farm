# Countryside Manor Farm

Single-page sample website for Countryside Manor Farm, a farm on Shaker Road in Franklin, Ohio (Warren County).

- **Phone:** (513) 264-5188
- **Address:** 1730 Shaker Rd, Franklin, OH 45005
- **Google listing:** "Countryside Manor Farm", category *Farm*, with address, phone and plus code (FP8G+55) only. It has no hours, website, photos or reviews.

## Structure

- `index.html`: the whole site. CSS is inline and there's no build step. The "manor" identity uses Gloock and Instrument Sans in hedgerow green, limestone, brick and gold, with light and dark themes.
- `img/`: stock photos from Unsplash (farmhouse, red barn, barn and silo, meadow cattle, field at sunrise, hay bales, grass at dusk). They're credited in the footer as representative, not the farm's own.

## Sections

1. Hero with call and text buttons
2. At-a-glance strip (address, phone, call first, Warren County)
3. The farm: honest, generic intro and a fact list
4. "Ask us about" cards, each opening a prefilled text
5. How it works: call or text, we confirm, come out
6. Text-a-note builder: pick a topic, add name and timing, and it opens a prefilled SMS to (513) 264-5188
7. Visit: address, "call before you come" note, directions, and an embedded Google map
8. FAQ
9. Contact band

## Data sources and honesty

Web, Facebook, Instagram and Bing searches found no other presence for this farm, only the Google listing. No products, prices, hours, history, family names or reviews were invented. All availability copy says "call or text". Swap in the farm's real photos, products and hours once the owner provides them.

The Sendra tracking script is included before `</body>`.
