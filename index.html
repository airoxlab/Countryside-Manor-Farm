The farm

Open country on Shaker Road.

Countryside Manor Farm sits at 1730 Shaker Road in Franklin, out in the fields and fence lines of Warren County.

Like most farms, what's going on here changes with the season, so we don't list things online that might not be ready. The best way to find out what's available, or to come out and see the place, is the simplest one: give us a call or send a text.

We're a short drive from Springboro, Lebanon, Mason and Franklin, and easy to reach from I-75.

Questions

Good to know.

Where is Countryside Manor Farm?

1730 Shaker Rd, Franklin, Ohio 45005, in Warren County between Dayton and Cincinnati. Open directions in Google Maps.

What are your hours?

We don't keep set public hours. Please call or text (513) 264-5188 before you come out and we'll find a time that works.

How do I find out what's available?

Call or text. What's on hand changes through the year, so a quick message is the most reliable way to get a current answer.

Can I order ahead?

Ask us. Let us know what you're after and when you need it, and we'll tell you what we can do.

Is texting okay?

Yes. Texting (513) 264-5188 works well, especially if you're asking during a busy part of the day. Leave your name so we know who we're talking to.
