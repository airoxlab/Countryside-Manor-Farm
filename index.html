Our farm

Fresh, simple, made with care.

Countryside Manor Farm sits at 1730 Shaker Road in Franklin, Ohio. We raise chickens for farm fresh eggs, grow our own produce and bake homemade bread.

We're a growing farm and we're grateful to every neighbor who has stopped by, placed an order or shared our page. The best way to order is a quick call or text, and we'll let you know what's ready.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I order?

Call or text (513) 264-5188, or use the order form above to send a text with everything filled in. We'll confirm and let you know when it's ready.

Where is Countryside Manor Farm?

1730 Shaker Rd, Franklin, Ohio 45005, in Warren County. Open directions in Google Maps.

Are eggs always available?

Our hens are laying about a dozen a day right now, and it's growing. Text us to reserve a dozen and we'll set it aside.

Which bread flavors do you have?

Sandwich bread ($5) and classic sourdough ($10) are our staples. Specialty sourdough like Jalapeño Cheddar, Pumpkin Slice and Peppermint Bark, plus our Gingerbread Loaf, are $12 and rotate with the season.

Where can I see what's new?

We post new bakes, holiday items and farm updates on our Facebook page. You can also email CountrysideManorFarm@yahoo.com.
